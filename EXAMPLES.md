# Two recorded research demonstrations

These are actual responses from fresh research runs on 6 October 2026 using Similar v0.1 beta. The prompts were selected to test a broad topic and a specific discovery. Findings were not supplied in advance. Each demonstration includes its prompt, complete response, official sources and verification limits.

These recordings predate beta.4's stricter grouping and completion requirements.
They have been preserved unchanged, not presented as tests of the revised instructions.

## 1. Libraries offer more than books

**Starting point:** Explore useful benefits of public libraries in Singapore.

**What it found:** Online courses, shared sewing and 3D-printing equipment, publications and learning communities. It then applied the shared-access principle outside libraries to public exercise facilities, keeping membership rules separate.

**Why it matters:** A broad topic can produce usable opportunities without requiring a personal success story first.

[Read the recorded response and source checks](examples/libraries-singapore.md).

## 2. Surplus food points to discounted journeys

**Starting point:** Too Good To Go sells surplus food to people willing to accept collection constraints.

**What it found:** Other surplus-food options, furniture with limited resale appeal, theatre seats approaching their deadline, and discounted vehicle rentals when a company needs a car or campervan moved between branches.

**Why it matters:** The connection extends beyond food: accepting a constraint can solve someone else's problem and reduce your cost. Each option identifies its market and important conditions.

[Read the recorded response and source checks](examples/surplus-opportunities.md).

## What these examples establish

They demonstrate researched connections and readable output in two runs. They are not customer outcomes, proof of savings, or a measured improvement over an ordinary prompt. No purchase or booking was made. See the [evaluation record and comparison method](EVALUATION.md).
