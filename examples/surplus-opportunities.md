# From surplus food to other opportunities

Recorded on 6 October 2026 using the v0.1 beta skill in a fresh Codex agent with live web research. This was a selected demonstration prompt, not a customer testimonial. No desired findings were supplied. Each run used one research agent; the release reviewer subsequently spot-checked decisive claims.

## Prompt

> Too Good To Go sells surprise bags of surplus food. Use Similar to find other useful opportunities from that idea, including outside food. I am flexible about timing, but explain simply and tell me where each opportunity is available.

## Recorded response

**Use your flexibility to take something its owner needs to clear, use soon, or move.** Too Good To Go does this with surplus food and a specified pickup window. The broader opportunity is to accept a constraint that costs you little but solves a problem for someone else. [Too Good To Go’s explanation](https://static.toogoodtogo.com/general-terms-conditions/en-us/index.html)

Since you haven’t specified a country, these are options in different markets, with availability beside each one.

**1. Choose discounted groceries before their best-before date**

Flashfood lets you buy listed grocery items approaching their best-before date and collect them in store. This gives you more control over what you buy than a surprise bag, while still helping a shop clear surplus. [How Flashfood works](https://help.flashfood.com/hc/en-us/articles/360049204914-What-is-Flashfood)

**Where:** Participating grocery stores in the **US and Canada**, searchable on its [store map](https://flashfood.com/en/locations).

**First step:** Check a store you already pass, then choose items you can use soon.

**Watch for:** Include the [service fee](https://help.flashfood.com/hc/en-us/articles/28110862514963-What-fees-do-I-pay) when comparing prices. Orders must be collected [on or before the items’ best-before dates](https://help.flashfood.com/hc/en-us/articles/360049202774-When-do-I-need-to-pick-up-my-order).

---

**2. Collect surplus food and useful household items for free**

Olio connects people with free food from local shops and things neighbours are giving away, including books, toys and toiletries. Here, your willingness to collect something helps someone avoid wasting it or keep it out of their way. [What Olio offers](https://olioapp.com/en/)

**Where:** The app is [available worldwide](https://help.olioapp.com/en/articles/12149055-where-and-how-can-i-access-olio), but nearby listings determine whether it is useful. Its business food-sharing programme has [partners across the UK](https://olioapp.com/en/help-your-students-eat-well-for-free/).

**First step:** Look at the free listings near you and request one thing you already need.

**Watch for:** Agree a collection time before travelling. Worldwide app access does not guarantee a local supply.

---

**3. Buy furniture that has lost its “new” status**

IKEA’s As-is department sells discontinued items, gently used products and former showroom displays. The connection to surplus food is useful stock that needs a different buyer: accepting limited choice or signs of use can unlock a lower price. [IKEA’s As-is guide](https://www.ikea.com/us/en/circular/second-hand/)

**Where:** This verified option is at **participating IKEA stores in the United States**. Other countries have their own programmes and rules.

**First step:** Browse your local store’s As-is stock for something on your shopping list. US online reservations require a free IKEA Family account and must be collected within 48 hours.

**Watch for:** Inspect the item and consider transport before paying. US As-is purchases are excluded from IKEA’s return policy; you can decline a reserved item after seeing it. [Reservation and return conditions](https://www.ikea.com/us/en/circular/second-hand/)

---

**4. Choose a theatre show from that day’s discounted seats**

A theatre seat, like today’s surplus meal, has a deadline: that performance cannot be sold tomorrow. TKTS offers discounted tickets for same-day performances and next-day matinees, making your flexible schedule useful. [TKTS programme](https://www.tdf.org/discount-ticket-programs/tkts-by-tdf/)

**Where:** **New York City**, at the Times Square and Lincoln Center booths. These are open to the public without a TDF membership. [Locations and eligibility](https://www.tdf.org/faq/)

**First step:** Check the official TKTS live listings, choose a few shows you would enjoy, then visit a booth during its opening hours.

**Watch for:** Your preferred show may be unavailable. Compare the total ticket price: there is an **US$8 service fee per ticket**, plus applicable theatre fees. [TKTS fees](https://www.tdf.org/faq/)

---

**5. Get a cheaper vehicle rental by moving it where it is needed**

Rental companies sometimes need a car or campervan moved between branches. Imoova offers discounted one-way rentals because you perform that move. This extends the surplus idea from “too much stock” to “useful stock in the wrong place.” [Why relocations are discounted](https://www.imoova.com/what-is-a-relocation)

**Where:** Routes in **Australia, New Zealand, the US, Canada and parts of Europe**. Its current listings include routes within France and Portugal, but individual routes and dates change. [Available relocations](https://www.imoova.com/deals)

**First step:** Search a journey you would already like to make, then check whether its pickup and return dates fit.

**Watch for:** The advertised daily rate is only part of the cost. Check booking fees, fuel, deposits, mileage limits and the supplier’s driver and insurance requirements. You must meet the return deadline, and the supplier can cancel if the vehicle no longer needs moving. [Costs](https://www.imoova.com/blog/costs-explained) · [Timing and cancellation limits](https://www.imoova.com/what-is-a-relocation)

## Source checks and limits

Research used official service pages, help centres and published terms linked in the response. The reviewer independently checked IKEA reservation and return rules, TKTS fees, and Imoova relocation conditions and costs. The direct TKTS programme page timed out during the run; its official search result and accessible FAQ supported the explanation. No listings were reserved, purchases made, or personal eligibility verified.

These are published-service checks as of the run date. Prices, eligibility and availability can change. The connections between opportunities are the assistant's reasoning, not provider endorsements. This run does not establish achieved savings or an advantage over a simpler prompt.
