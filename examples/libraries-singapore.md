# Libraries in Singapore

Recorded on 6 October 2026 using the v0.1 beta skill in a fresh Codex agent with live web research. This was a selected demonstration prompt, not a customer testimonial. No desired findings were supplied. Each run used one research agent; the release reviewer subsequently spot-checked decisive claims.

## Prompt

> Use Similar on public libraries in Singapore. I want useful things beyond borrowing books. Explain simply.

## Recorded response

**Start with the library’s online courses and shared equipment.** They let you learn or try a hobby before paying for a subscription or buying a machine.

I’ll focus on Singapore’s National Library Board (NLB), with one related idea outside libraries.

For member services, check your [membership category](https://www.nlb.gov.sg/main/services/library-membership/types-of-membership): basic membership is free for Singaporeans; permanent residents normally pay a one-time S$10.69 registration fee; eligible foreign residents normally pay S$43.60 yearly or S$3.82 monthly, plus registration. Some waivers apply.

---

**1. Learn a practical skill from home**

NLB provides LinkedIn Learning courses in software, business and creative skills at no extra course charge. You don’t need a LinkedIn profile. [NLB’s guide](https://eresources.nlb.gov.sg/main/Open/FAQ/6)

**Example:** Search for an Excel course and practise with a household budget.

**First step:** Open [NLB eResources](https://eresources.nlb.gov.sg/main/Browse?browseBy=subject&filter=1&page=1), choose LinkedIn Learning and follow the library sign-in instructions.

**Watch for:** Completion certificates through this library service do not include your name.

---

**2. Try sewing or 3D printing without buying the equipment**

[MakeIT](https://www.nlb.gov.sg/main/services/MakeIT-at-Libraries) offers beginner workshops, equipment and materials free for NLB members. This is a practical way to find out whether you enjoy making things before investing in tools.

**First step:** Choose a Starter Session for the equipment you want to learn.

**Watch for:** Independent equipment use requires the relevant Starter Session certification. The [equipment booking page](https://www.nlb.gov.sg/main/services/MakeIT-at-Libraries/Self-Service-Information/Book-Equipment) also requires Singpass. Check the workshop’s age requirements and available places.

---

**3. Read newspapers and magazines through the library**

NLB’s PressReader access covers publications from many countries. It can be useful for following overseas news or exploring a magazine before buying it. [NLB’s resource listing](https://eresources.nlb.gov.sg/main/Browse?browseBy=subject&filter=1&page=1)

**First step:** Find PressReader in NLB eResources and search for a publication you actually want to read.

**Watch for:** Availability varies by title. Some, including The Straits Times through PressReader, are available only inside libraries.

---

**4. Find people to learn alongside**

NLB’s [LearnX Communities](https://www.nlb.gov.sg/main/site/learnx/explore-communities) bring people together around interests such as technology, arts and sustainability. The useful part is having people to exchange ideas with, especially when learning alone gets tiring.

**First step:** Browse the community directory, pick one interest and check its next session through GoLibrary.

**Watch for:** Check the individual session’s registration rules, experience level and availability.

---

**5. Apply the same idea outside libraries: shared exercise facilities**

The connection is shared access: just as MakeIT lets you use equipment without owning it, ActiveSG lets you use exercise facilities by paying for a visit. Its listed adult gym entry is S$2.50 for Singapore citizens and permanent residents, or S$3.30 at the standard rate. [Official rates](https://www.activesgcircle.gov.sg/facilities/individual-rates)

**First step:** Check a nearby ActiveSG gym and its entry arrangements before considering a longer membership.

**Watch for:** This is a separate service. An NLB membership does not provide ActiveSG access.

## Source checks and limits

Official NLB pages and its LinkedIn Learning guide support the library services and their conditions. The ActiveSG rate page supports the separate shared-facility example. The reviewer independently checked MakeIT access, the LinkedIn guide and gym rates. Membership eligibility, live session availability and signed-in access were not tested.

These are published-service checks as of the run date. Prices, eligibility and availability can change. The connections between opportunities are the assistant's reasoning, not provider endorsements. This run does not establish achieved savings or an advantage over a simpler prompt.
